### Guidance scale and number of sampling steps

**Setup.** All images were generated with the group's shared protocol (`m2-v1`, diffusers 0.42.1): Stable Diffusion 1.5 in half precision with its CLIP text encoder, DDIM (eta 0), leading timestep spacing and the safety checker on. Each sweep changed one setting away from the anchor (30 steps, guidance 7.5). The starting noise for a caption was drawn on the CPU from seed = 100000 × seed_id + caption_idx, so every setting painted each caption from identical noise and settings can be compared caption by caption. The guidance sweep (3, 5, 7.5, 10, 12) used all 500 captions of `dev_500` with one seed; the step sweep (10, 20, 30, 50) used its first 200. The first 100 captions were painted a second time (seed_id 1) at every guidance value to measure diversity. The final comparison on the test captions used three seeds per caption. 20 of the 3,600 dev images (0.6%) were blacked out by the safety checker and left out.

**Measures.** Object recall is the share of the labelled objects in the captions (559 in `dev_500`) that appear in the image, judged by Faster R-CNN v2 (class score ≥ 0.5) and OWL-ViT ("a photo of a {class}", score ≥ 0.1); an object counts as present if either judge finds it. On the real photos behind the same captions the judges find 98.9% [98.0, 99.6] of the objects, the ceiling for this measure. Image quality is measured by KID (× 1000), FID and the Inception Score against 2,000 COCO photos that share no photo with any caption set, and text alignment by CLIPScore (ViT-L/14). FID and the Inception Score depend on the number of images, so each is reported next to the real-photo floor, the score of the real photos behind the same captions at the same size: with 500 images real photos get FID 78.0, KID 0.05 [-0.26, 0.47] and IS 31.12; with 200, FID 132.8, KID -0.02 and IS 24.74. The Inception Score rates how confidently an ImageNet classifier labels each image and how varied the labels are; it was designed for single-object ImageNet-like images and says less about COCO scenes, so it is reported for completeness; higher is not better here when it exceeds the real photos' score. Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals over captions; differences between settings are paired on the same captions and noise. FID and the Inception Score of a single set carry no interval: for the Inception Score, a resample repeats images, which the score reads as lost variety, so only paired differences, where that shift falls on both sides, get one.

**Guidance.** Raising guidance from 3 to 12 moved object recall from 91.8% to 93.7%; against the anchor (7.5, 93.7%) the paired differences were -2.0 points [-4.0, -0.2] at 3 and +0.0 [-1.6, +1.5] at 12 (a detectable change at 3, no detectable change at 12). CLIPScore went from 26.00 to 26.85 (paired change at 3: -0.75 [-0.97, -0.52]; at 12: +0.10 [-0.07, +0.27]). KID rose from 1.68 to 6.03 (paired change at 3: -2.71 [-3.55, -1.96]; at 12: +1.63 [+0.95, +2.31]), against a real-photo floor of 0.05; FID went from 81.8 to 86.9 (floor 78.0). The Inception Score fell from 32.66 to 30.64 (paired change at 12: -0.95 [-3.06, +0.98]; real photos 31.12). For diversity, the LPIPS between a caption's two seeds rose from 0.747 [0.734, 0.760] at guidance 3 to 0.773 [0.760, 0.787] at 12, while their CLIP image distance fell from 0.223 [0.206, 0.241] to 0.151 [0.136, 0.167]. The two measures disagree: LPIPS responds to texture and fine detail, CLIP distance to content, so higher guidance made the two pictures more alike in what they show while more different in detail. Figure 1 shows all six measures; Figure 3 shows captions whose object is judged differently at 3 and at 12.

**Steps.** Against 30 steps on the same 200 captions, recall changed by +0.9 points [-1.4, +3.6] at 10 steps, +0.5 [-0.9, +1.9] at 20 and +0.5 [-0.9, +1.9] at 50; KID changed by -0.42 [-1.96, +0.80], +0.42 [-0.43, +1.31] and +0.61 [-0.36, +1.61] (real-photo floor at 200 images: -0.02), and the Inception Score by -3.72, +1.55 and -0.24. Because both sides of each comparison start from the same noise, the comparison is paired: the 10-step interval rules out a recall loss of more than 1.4 points, the 20-step interval rules out a recall loss of more than 0.9 points. One image took 1.9 s at 10 steps, 5.5 s at 30 and 8.4 s at 50 on a Tesla T4 (Figure 2).

**Choice.** By rules fixed before the results were seen (raise recall without a detectable KID cost; otherwise keep the anchor; fewest steps that lose less than 5 points of recall), the setting carried to the test captions is **guidance 7.5, 10 steps** (`ddim-leading-s10-g7.5`). It is evaluated on `eval_500`, which no choice has used.

**Test captions (`eval_500`, 3 seeds per caption).** The anchor found 94.4% [93.0, 95.6] of 1703 labelled-object instances, with KID 4.66 [4.01, 5.92], FID 84.9 and IS 35.73 (averages of the per-seed values, 500 images each) and CLIPScore 26.70; the real photos behind these captions get FID 75.1, KID -0.16 and IS 33.58. The chosen setting found 94.3%: a paired change of +0.0 points [-1.0, +1.0] (no detectable change), with KID -0.85 [-1.44, -0.32] (a detectable change), IS -3.30 [-5.54, -1.90] (a detectable change) and CLIPScore +0.02 [-0.10, +0.14] (no detectable change), at 1.7 s per image instead of 4.7 s. Per seed, the recall change was -0.9, +0.3, +0.5 points. On the Inception Score the anchor (35.73) sits above the real photos (33.58) and the chosen setting (32.55) closer to them, so its lower score does not mean worse images: with KID, it suggests the chosen setting's images are, if anything, closer to real photos.
